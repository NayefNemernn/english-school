Present Perfect

⏱ 15 min

📐 Structure: have/has + past participle

Rule
Use Present Perfect to talk about past experiences without a specific time, or things that affect the present.
للحديث عن تجارب بدون وقت محدد، أو أشياء مؤثرة على الحاضر
SubjectFormExample
I / You / We / Theyhave + past participleI have visited Paris.
He / She / Ithas + past participleShe has worked here for 3 years.
  • I have eaten sushi before.لقد أكلت سوشي من قبل (تجربة)
  • She has never been to London.هي لم تذهب أبداً إلى لندن
  • Have you seen this film?هل شاهدت هذا الفيلم؟
Common words used with Present Perfect: ever, never, already, just, yet, for, since

Conditionals — "If" sentences

⏱ 15 min

📐 Type 1: Real / Possible Conditions

Formula
If + Present Simple → will + verb
إذا (حاضر) → سوف (مستقبل)
  • If I study, I will pass the exam.إذا درست، سأنجح في الامتحان
  • If it rains, we will stay home.إذا أمطرت، سنبقى في البيت
  • If she works hard, she will succeed.إذا عملت بجد، ستنجح

Speaking Practice — Real Conversations

⏱ 10 min

Read these conversations out loud. Practice them with a friend or say them alone!

🍽️ Conversation 1: Ordering Food

  • Waiter: Good evening! What would you like?
  • You: I would like a pizza and water, please.
  • Waiter: Of course. Anything else?
  • You: No, thank you. That's all.

📍 Conversation 2: Asking for Directions

  • You: Excuse me, where is the nearest pharmacy?
  • Person: Go straight, then turn left. It's next to the school.
  • You: Thank you very much!
  • Person: You're welcome. Have a good day!
