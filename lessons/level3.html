Vocabulary — Adjectives

⏱ 15 min

Adjectives describe nouns (people, places, things). They come BEFORE the noun in English.

Word order tip: In English: adjective + noun → "a beautiful city" — not "a city beautiful".
في الإنجليزية الصفة تأتي قبل الاسم (عكس العربية أحياناً)

Grammar — Past Simple Tense

⏱ 20 min

📐 When do we use it?

Rule
Use Past Simple to talk about completed actions in the past — things that are finished.
نستخدمه للأحداث المنتهية في الماضي
  • I visited Beirut last year.زرت بيروت السنة الماضية
  • She studied for the exam yesterday.هي درست للامتحان أمس
  • They watched a film last night.هم شاهدوا فيلماً الليلة الماضية

📐 Regular Verbs — الأفعال المنتظمة

Rule
For regular verbs, add "-ed" to the end. The same form for ALL subjects.
أضف "-ed" للفعل — نفس الشكل لجميع الضمائر
Base verbPast SimpleExample
watchwatchedI watched TV last night.
workworkedShe worked late yesterday.
studystudiedThey studied all day.
livelivedHe lived in Paris.
visitvisitedWe visited our family.
Special spelling: If the verb ends in "e" → just add "d": live→lived, love→loved
If it ends in consonant+y → change y to i and add "ed": study→studied

⚠️ Irregular Verbs — الأفعال الشاذة

Important
Some verbs don't follow the "-ed" rule. You must memorize them!
بعض الأفعال لا تتبع القاعدة — يجب حفظها
BasePastMeaning

drinkdrankيشرب

seesawيرى
comecameيأتي

❌ Negative & ❓ Questions

Rule
Use did not (didn't) for negatives and Did at the start for questions. The verb stays in base form!
استخدم "didn't" للنفي و"Did" للأسئلة — الفعل يبقى بشكله الأصلي
  • I didn't go to school yesterday.لم أذهب إلى المدرسة
  • She didn't eat breakfast.هي لم تأكل الفطور
  • Did you study last night? — Yes, I did.هل درست الليلة الماضية؟

Review & Final Quiz

⏱ 5 min

📝 Quick Summary

  • Adjectives come before the noun: "a beautiful city"
  • Regular past: verb + -ed (watched, studied, worked)
  • Irregular past: go→went, eat→ate, have→had
  • Negative: didn't + base verb
  • Question: Did + subject + base verb?
