Grammar — The Verb "To Be"

⏱ 20 min

📐 The Rule — القاعدة

Rule
The verb "To Be" changes depending on who we are talking about (the subject).
الفعل "To Be" يتغير حسب الضمير — أنا، أنت، هو، هي...
Subject (الضمير) Verb (الفعل) Short form Example
I (أنا)amI'mI am a student.
You (أنت)areYou'reYou are my friend.
He (هو)isHe'sHe is a teacher.
She (هي)isShe'sShe is happy.
It (هو/هي للأشياء)isIt'sIt is a book.
We (نحن)areWe'reWe are friends.
They (هم)areThey'reThey are students.
🧠 How to remember: Only "I" uses am. He/She/It uses is. Everything else uses are.
فقط "I" تستخدم "am"، وHe/She/It تستخدم "is"، والباقي يستخدم "are"

❌ Negative Form — النفي

Rule
To make a negative sentence, add "not" after the verb "to be".
لجعل الجملة نافية، أضف "not" بعد الفعل
  • I am not tired. (I'm not tired)لست متعباً
  • She is not happy. (She isn't happy)هي ليست سعيدة
  • They are not students. (They aren't students)هم ليسوا طلاباً

❓ Question Form — السؤال

Rule
To ask a question, move "To Be" to the BEGINNING of the sentence.
للسؤال، ضع الفعل في بداية الجملة
  • Are you a student? — Yes, I am. / No, I'm not.
  • Is she your sister? — Yes, she is. / No, she isn't.
  • Are they from Lebanon? — Yes, they are.
