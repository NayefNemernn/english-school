Grammar — Simple Present Tense

⏱ 20 min

📐 When do we use it? — متى نستخدمه؟

Rule
We use the Simple Present to talk about: habits, routines, facts, and things that are always true.
نستخدمه للعادات والروتين والحقائق العامة
  • I drink coffee every morning. (habit)عادة يومية
  • The sun rises in the east. (fact)حقيقة
  • She works in a hospital. (routine)روتين

📐 How to form it — كيف نكوّنه

Important Rule — قاعدة مهمة
With He / She / It → add "s" or "es" to the verb.
مع He / She / It أضف "s" أو "es" للفعل
SubjectVerb (eat)Example
IeatI eat rice every day.
YoueatYou eat a lot of bread.
He / She / IteatsShe eats fish on Friday.
WeeatWe eat together.
TheyeatThey eat late at night.
Special spelling: go → goes | watch → watches | study → studies
بعض الأفعال لها إملاء خاص عند إضافة S

❌ Negative — النفي

Rule
Use do not (don't) or does not (doesn't) before the verb.
استخدم "don't" مع I/You/We/They و"doesn't" مع He/She/It
  • I don't eat meat.لا آكل اللحم
  • She doesn't drink coffee.هي لا تشرب القهوة
  • They don't work on Sundays.هم لا يعملون الأحد

❓ Questions — الأسئلة

Rule
Use Do or Does at the beginning of the question.
استخدم "Do" أو "Does" في بداية السؤال
  • Do you like coffee? — Yes, I do. / No, I don't.
  • Does she work here? — Yes, she does. / No, she doesn't.
  • Do they speak Arabic? — Yes, they do.
