💡 What is the Past Continuous? The Past Continuous (also called the Past Progressive) describes an action that was in progress at a specific moment in the past. Think of it as a "movie" — an action that was playing/happening when something else occurred.

الماضي المستمر يصف حدثاً كان جارياً في لحظة معينة في الماضي — مثل فيلم كان يُعرض عندما حدث شيء آخر.

Compare:
Simple Past → "I read a book." (completed action — قرأت كتاباً وانتهيت)
Past Continuous → "I was reading a book." (action in progress — كنت أقرأ كتاباً [في تلك اللحظة])

Time Expressions & Useful Verbs

⏱ 10 min

⏰ Signal Words — كلمات تشير إلى الماضي المستمر

These words are strong clues that a Past Continuous tense is needed. Memorise them!

while (بينما) when (عندما) as (بينما) at that moment (في تلك اللحظة) at ... o'clock yesterday at this time yesterday (في هذا الوقت أمس) all day (طوال اليوم) all morning / all night from ... to ... (من ... إلى ...) still (لا يزال)
📌 "while" vs "when"
while → usually followed by Past Continuous: "While I was sleeping, the phone rang."
when → usually followed by Simple Past: "I was sleeping when the phone rang."

Both sentences have the SAME meaning — only the word order changes.
📌 Examples with signal words:
I was watching TV at 9pm last night.
While she was cooking, he arrived.
They were studying all morning.

⚠️ Stative Verbs — أفعال الحالة (cannot use -ing) Some verbs describe a STATE (not an action) and are NOT normally used in continuous tenses. Common stative verbs: know, believe, understand, like, love, hate, want, need, see, hear, seem, appear, belong, own, have (= possess).

✗ "I was knowing the answer." → ✓ "I knew the answer." (Simple Past)
✗ "She was liking the movie." → ✓ "She liked the movie." (Simple Past)

Grammar — The Complete Guide

⏱ 20 min

📐 When do we use Past Continuous? — متى نستخدمه؟

Use 1
Action in progress at a past moment
"At 8pm last night, I was watching TV."
حدث كان جارياً في لحظة معينة بالماضي
Use 2
Interrupted action (when + Simple Past)
"I was reading when she called."
حدث كان مستمراً ثم قاطعه حدث آخر
Use 3
Two simultaneous actions (while)
"While she was cooking, he was cleaning."
حدثان كانا يجريان في نفس الوقت
Use 4
Background in stories / narratives
"The sun was shining. Birds were singing…"
وصف الخلفية في القصص
Use 5
Expressing annoyance (always/constantly)
"He was always interrupting me!"
التعبير عن الانزعاج من عادة متكررة

🔵 Was / Were — Choosing the Right Helper Verb

Core Rule
Past Continuous always uses was or were as a helper verb + the -ing form of the main verb.
الماضي المستمر يستخدم دائماً was أو were كفعل مساعد + المصدر مضافاً إليه -ing
WAS
I  •  He  •  She  •  It
(singular subjects)
WERE
You  •  We  •  They
(plural subjects)
SubjectHelper verbExample
IwasI was sleeping.
He / She / ItwasShe was cooking.
YouwereYou were talking.
WewereWe were studying.
TheywereThey were running.
Memory tip: Same rule as Simple Past "To Be":
WAS = I, he, she, it (singular)  |  WERE = you, we, they (plural)

📝 -ing Spelling Rules — قواعد إضافة -ing

General Rule
Add -ing to the base form of the verb — but watch out for spelling changes!
نضيف -ing للفعل الأصلي — لكن انتبه للتغييرات الإملائية!
🔤 Vowel Letters
A E I O U
Only these 5 letters are vowels.
🔠 Consonant Letters
B C D F G H J K L M
N P Q R S T V W X Y Z
All letters that are NOT vowels.

⚠️ 4 Spelling Rules for -ing:

Rule 1 — Most verbs: just add -ing
work → working  |  eat → eating
talk → talking  |  play → playing
cook → cooking  |  read → reading
Rule 2 — Ends in silent -e: drop -e, add -ing
write → writing  |  drive → driving
dance → dancing  |  live → living
take → taking  |  use → using
Rule 3 — Short vowel + single consonant: double it, add -ing
run → running  |  sit → sitting
swim → swimming  |  stop → stopping
get → getting  |  cut → cutting
Rule 4 — Ends in -ie: change -ie to -y, add -ing
lie → lying  |  die → dying
tie → tying
🔍 Rule 3 — How to identify "short vowel + single consonant":
Look at the last 3 letters. If the pattern is: consonant → vowel → consonant (CVC) in a one-syllable word, double the final consonant.

✓ r-u-n (CVC) → running
✓ s-i-t (CVC) → sitting
✗ r-e-a-d (ends in 2 consonant-like letters after vowel cluster) → reading (no double)
Base Verb-ing FormRule UsedFull Example
workworkingRule 1I was working all day.
studystudyingRule 1She was studying English.
writewritingRule 2 (drop -e)He was writing a letter.
drivedrivingRule 2 (drop -e)She was driving home.
runrunningRule 3 (double n)They were running fast.

✅ Affirmative Form — الجملة المثبتة

Subject + was / were + verb-ing + rest of sentence
  • I was sleeping when you called.كنت نائماً عندما اتصلت
  • He was cooking dinner at 7pm.كان يطبخ العشاء الساعة السابعة
  • She was studying all morning.كانت تدرس طوال الصباح
  • We were watching a film when the lights went out.كنا نشاهد فيلماً عندما انقطع الضوء
  • They were playing football while it was raining.كانوا يلعبون كرة القدم بينما كانت تمطر

❌ Negative Form — النفي

Subject + was not (wasn't) / were not (weren't) + verb-ing
  • I wasn't sleeping — I was reading.لم أكن نائماً — كنت أقرأ
  • She wasn't working at that time.هي لم تكن تعمل في ذلك الوقت
  • They weren't listening to the teacher.هم لم يكونوا يستمعون للمعلم
  • We weren't expecting visitors.نحن لم نكن نتوقع زواراً

🚫 Common Mistake — خطأ شائع:

✗ Wrong
I didn't sleeping.
She wasn't study.
✓ Correct
I wasn't sleeping.
She wasn't studying.

For negatives, use wasn't/weren't (NOT "didn't") + the -ing form (NOT the base form).

❓ Question Form — صيغة السؤال

Yes/No Questions:

Was / Were + subject + verb-ing + ?

WH- Questions (who, what, when, where, why, how):

WH- word + was / were + subject + verb-ing + ?
QuestionShort Answer (Yes)Short Answer (No)
Was he sleeping?Yes, he was.No, he wasn't.
Was she working late?Yes, she was.No, she wasn't.
Were you listening?Yes, I was.No, I wasn't.
Were they playing?Yes, they were.No, they weren't.
What were you doing?I was reading a book.
Where was she going?She was going to the market.
Why were they running?They were running to catch the bus.
📌 Short Answer reminder:
"Were you studying?" → "Yes, I was." (NOT "Yes, I were.")
"Was he cooking?" → "No, he wasn't." (NOT "No, he weren't.")

⚡ Past Continuous vs Simple Past — الفرق الأساسي

The Key Pattern — النمط الأساسي
When one action was in progress (Past Continuous) and another action interrupted it (Simple Past), we use BOTH tenses together.
عندما كان حدث ما مستمراً (ماضي مستمر) وقاطعه حدث آخر مفاجئ (ماضي بسيط)، نستخدم الزمنين معاً.

📊 The Timeline Concept — مفهوم الخط الزمني:

  Past ←————————————————→ Now
       [~~~ was reading ~~~]
                 ↓ rang (Simple Past)
       "I was reading when the phone rang."

Two sentence patterns — نمطان للجملة:

Pattern A — "when" at the middle
[Past Continuous] + when + [Simple Past]

"I was cooking when he arrived."
"She was sleeping when the alarm rang."
"They were watching TV when the power went off."
Pattern B — "while" at the start
While + [Past Continuous], + [Simple Past]

"While I was cooking, he arrived."
"While she was sleeping, the alarm rang."
"While they were watching TV, the power went off."
📌 Both sentences mean the SAME thing!
The only difference is whether "when" or "while" comes first.

Two simultaneous actions — حدثان في نفس الوقت:

When two longer actions happen at the same time, use Past Continuous for both:

  • While she was cooking, he was cleaning.بينما كانت تطبخ، كان هو ينظف
  • I was talking on the phone while she was watching TV.كنت أتحدث بالهاتف بينما كانت تشاهد التلفاز

Full Comparison — مقارنة شاملة:

Simple PastPast Continuous
Completed action
حدث منتهٍ تماماً		 Action in progress at a moment
حدث كان مستمراً في لحظة ما
I read a book. (finished) I was reading a book. (in progress)
She called me. (short, complete) She was calling me. (at that moment)
They watched TV last night. At 9pm they were watching TV.
Signal words: yesterday, ago, last… Signal words: while, when, at that moment…

🚫 Common Mistakes — أخطاء شائعة يجب تجنبها

Mistake 1 — Using "didn't" instead of "wasn't/weren't":

✗ Wrong
I didn't sleeping.
✓ Correct
I wasn't sleeping.

Mistake 2 — Using base verb instead of -ing after was/were:

✗ Wrong
She was cook dinner.
✓ Correct
She was cooking dinner.

Mistake 3 — Using wrong helper verb (was/were):

✗ Wrong
They was playing football.
✓ Correct
They were playing football.

Mistake 4 — Using Past Continuous for stative verbs:

✗ Wrong
I was knowing the answer.
✓ Correct
I knew the answer. (Simple Past)

Mistake 5 — Forgetting to double the consonant in -ing:

✗ Wrong
He was runing. / She was siting.
✓ Correct
He was running. / She was sitting.

Mistake 6 — Forgetting to drop -e before -ing:

✗ Wrong
She was writeing. / He was driveing.
✓ Correct
She was writing. / He was driving.
