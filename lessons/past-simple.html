⏰ Signal Words — كلمات تشير إلى الماضي

These words tell you that the sentence is in the Simple Past. When you see them, use the past tense!

yesterday last night last week last month last year ... ago (two days ago) in + year (in 2020) this morning the day before yesterday when I was young once upon a time in the past at that time then just now
📌 Examples with signal words:
I went to school yesterday.
She worked here five years ago.
They met last Monday.

Grammar — The Complete Guide

📐 When do we use Simple Past? — متى نستخدمه؟

Use 1
Completed past actions
"I visited Beirut last year."
أحداث منتهية في الماضي
Use 2
Series of past events
"I woke up, ate breakfast and left."
سلسلة من الأحداث الماضية
Use 3
Habits in the past
"He played football every day as a child."
عادات ماضية (لم تعد موجودة)
Use 4
Facts about the past
"Einstein was a scientist."
حقائق تاريخية
Use 5
Storytelling & narratives
"Once upon a time, a king lived..."
القصص والحكايات

✅ Affirmative Form — الجملة المثبتة

Subject + Past Verb + rest of sentence
Important Note
The past form is the SAME for all subjects — I, you, he, she, it, we, they all use the same form.
شكل الماضي واحد لجميع الضمائر — لا تغيير!
  • I worked yesterday.أنا عملت أمس
  • She worked yesterday.هي عملت أمس
  • They worked yesterday.هم عملوا أمس

📝 Regular Verbs — الأفعال المنتظمة (add -ed)

General Rule
Regular verbs form the past by adding "-ed" to the base form.
الأفعال المنتظمة تكوّن الماضي بإضافة "-ed" للفعل الأصلي
🔤 Vowel Letters — حروف العلة
A E I O U
Only these 5 letters are vowels.
هذه الحروف الخمسة فقط هي حروف العلة
🔠 Consonant Letters — الحروف الساكنة
B C D F G H J K L M
N P Q R S T V W X Y Z
All letters that are NOT vowels.
كل الحروف التي ليست حروف علة
📌 What is a "Short Vowel + Single Consonant"? — ما معنى هذا؟

A short vowel is when a vowel (a, e, i, o, u) makes a short sound — not a long sound. A single consonant means there is only ONE consonant letter at the end of the word (not two).

✓ Applies Rule 4:
stop  →  s·to·p  (short o + single p)
plan  →  p·la·n  (short a + single n)
fit  →  f·i·t  (short i + single t)
✗ Does NOT apply:
talk  →  ends in two consonants (lk)
rain  →  ends in two vowels + n
visit  →  two syllables (not one)

⚠️ 4 Spelling Rules — 4 قواعد إملائية:

Rule 1 — Most verbs: add -ed
walk → walked  |  talk → talked
clean → cleaned  |  help → helped
Rule 2 — Ends in -e: add -d only
live → lived  |  love → loved
dance → danced  |  use → used
Rule 3 — Consonant + y → change y to i, add -ed
study → studied  |  try → tried
carry → carried  |  cry → cried
Rule 4 — Short vowel + single consonant: double it, add -ed
stop → stopped  |  plan → planned
shop → shopped  |  fit → fitted
🔍 Vowel + y → no change! (Rule 3 exception) If the verb ends in vowel + y, just add -ed normally:
play → played  |  stay → stayed  |  enjoy → enjoyed

🔵 Was / Were — Past of "To Be"

Rule
The verb "To Be" has a special past form. "Am/Is" → was. "Are" → were.
الفعل "To Be" له شكل خاص في الماضي: am/is تصبح "was" و are تصبح "were"
SubjectPresentPastExample
IamwasI was tired yesterday.
He / She / ItiswasShe was at home last night.
YouarewereYou were very kind.
WearewereWe were in school.
TheyarewereThey were happy.
Memory tip: WAS = singular (I, he, she, it)  |  WERE = plural (you, we, they)

❌ Negative Form — النفي

For action verbs (go, eat, work...):

Subject + did not (didn't) + BASE verb ← NOT past form!

For "To Be" (was/were):

Subject + was not (wasn't) / were not (weren't)
  • I didn't go to work yesterday.لم أذهب إلى العمل أمس
  • She didn't eat breakfast this morning.هي لم تأكل الفطور هذا الصباح
  • They didn't watch the match.هم لم يشاهدوا المباراة
  • He wasn't at school.هو لم يكن في المدرسة
  • We weren't ready.نحن لم نكن مستعدين

🚫 Most Common Mistake — أكثر خطأ شائع:

✗ Wrong
I didn't went to school.
She didn't ate breakfast.
✓ Correct
I didn't go to school.
She didn't eat breakfast.

After "didn't", ALWAYS use the base (infinitive) form of the verb — never the past form!

❓ Question Form — صيغة السؤال

For action verbs (Yes/No questions):

Did + subject + BASE verb + ?

For "To Be" questions:

Was / Were + subject + rest of sentence + ?

WH- Questions (who, what, when, where, why, how):

WH- word + did + subject + BASE verb + ?
